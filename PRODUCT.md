# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating a software engineer for full-time remote roles (Germany), roles in Bangalore, and freelance work; secondarily, peer engineers judging craft. They arrive from a resume link, GitHub or LinkedIn, skim fast, and want to know who this is, what was built, and how to get in touch.

## Product Purpose
Personal portfolio of Maarcus Reniero L (Chennai, IN). It presents projects with case studies (zentro, life os, nyx, scanforge and others), experience and resume, writing, and a way to make contact. Success: a visitor understands the work within seconds and reaches out.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Next.js app, existing routes: home, projects (+ case studies), experience, resume, stack, about, blog, contact (form), privacy.
- Copy lives in `src/lib/content.ts`; bracketed placeholders are intentionally visible until real content exists.
- Kept: projects and case studies, experience and resume, blog, contact form.
- Cut (confirmed): terminal, command palette, hero WebGL scene, page transitions, ask-portfolio demo and other interactive extras.
- Added Nov 2026: Lenis smooth scrolling and motion-based entrances (first screen rises in order, sections reveal on scroll, list rows animate when filtered; all off for reduced motion), with no loader; vim-style keys (j/k, gg/G, /, Enter) and search/filters on Projects and Writing; privacy page removed; Stack is in the nav.

## Brand Commitments
User wants a very minimal looking website. Name, domain (maarcus.dev) and existing copy stay.

## Product Principles
- Content over chrome: every element must earn its place.
- Fast to scan, easy to contact.
- Restraint is the identity; no decorative effects.
