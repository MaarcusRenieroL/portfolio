# my plan for ai, cloud and security

i am preparing for a masters in ai, cloud and cybersecurity. before that, i want the portfolio to be honest about what i can already do and what i still have to learn.

## what already counts

- **ai:** nyx runs a local assistant on ollama, with citations and streamed tokens.
- **cloud:** life os runs as a docker stack behind nginx, and aws is part of my work at ust healthproof.
- **security:** ccna and ccnp for the network side, and secrets generation, a password vault and clipboard redaction in my own projects.

## what comes next

the [roadmap](/roadmap) lists every milestone with a status. nothing is marked done unless a project, a job or a certificate backs it up.

## how i will write it up
